## Description
Some people can spend an entire evening alone without feeling like anything is missing. Are they lonely, antisocial — or something else entirely?

In this episode of Why We Become, we explore the psychology of solitude: why being alone and feeling lonely are not the same thing, why some thoughtful, curious people need uninterrupted time, how social interaction can become emotional work, and how to tell a healthy preference for quiet from withdrawal driven by fear.

There is no universal rule that intelligent people prefer to be alone — but understanding why some people enjoy their own company can change how you see yourself and the people around you.

Think deeper. Live better. Become more.
